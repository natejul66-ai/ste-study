1. Music and Arts

Classical Foundations: Western Music and Greco-Roman Arts in Filipino Cultural Context

The study of classical foundations helps learners understand how Western musical and artistic traditions developed and how these traditions can be connected to Filipino cultural references.

The lesson emphasizes listening, observation, comparison, cultural connection, and guided creative work.

Western Classical Music

Western classical music refers to a broad tradition of music that developed primarily in Europe and includes different historical periods, styles, forms, and approaches to composition.

Key idea:

When studying classical music, focus on what you hear, such as melody, rhythm, dynamics, instruments, texture, and overall character.

Key Musical Elements

Elements that help you listen to and describe music.

01

Melody

The organized sequence of musical notes that forms a recognizable musical idea.

02

Rhythm

The pattern of sounds and silences and how musical beats are organized.

03

Dynamics

Changes in the loudness or softness of music.

04

Texture

How different melodic or musical lines interact with one another.

05

Timbre

The quality or tone color that makes one instrument or voice sound different from another.

06

Form

The overall structure or organization of a musical composition.

2. Greco-Roman Arts

Foundations of ancient Greek and Roman artistic traditions.

Greek Art

Ancient Greek art is known for its interest in human figures, proportion, balance, harmony, and idealized representations of people and the natural world.

Roman Art

Roman art was strongly influenced by Greek traditions while also developing its own approaches to architecture, sculpture, painting, and public art.

01

Sculpture

Three-dimensional artworks representing people, gods, historical figures, or other subjects.

02

Architecture

Buildings and structures characterized by deliberate design, proportion, function, and decoration.

03

Mosaics

Images or patterns created by arranging small pieces of materials such as stone or tile.

04

Pottery

Ceramic objects that can provide information about ancient life, beliefs, activities, and artistic practices.

3. Filipino Cultural Context

Connecting classical foundations with Filipino culture.

Studying Western classical foundations does not mean treating them separately from Filipino culture. Learners can examine how artistic ideas, musical practices, forms, and visual elements can be observed, adapted, or interpreted within Filipino cultural contexts.

01

Observe

Identify noticeable features in a musical or visual artwork.

02

Listen

Pay attention to musical elements such as rhythm, melody, dynamics, and instruments.

03

Compare

Look for similarities and differences between classical traditions and Filipino cultural references.

04

Create

Apply observed ideas through guided creative activities.

Remember: Cultural comparison is about understanding relationships and differences, not deciding that one culture is automatically better than another.

4. Music and Arts Quick Review

Important concepts to remember.

Western Classical Music: A broad European musical tradition containing many periods, styles, forms, and techniques.
Melody: An organized sequence of musical notes.
Rhythm: The organization of beats, sounds, and silences.
Dynamics: Changes in musical loudness or softness.
Greco-Roman Art: Artistic traditions associated with ancient Greece and Rome.
Filipino Cultural Context: Connecting classical ideas to Filipino cultural references and experiences.

5. PE and Health

Responsible Parenthood and Family Planning for Family Wellness

Responsible parenthood involves recognizing the responsibilities involved in raising and caring for children while supporting the overall wellness of the family.

Family planning involves making informed decisions concerning the timing and spacing of children and considering the needs, responsibilities, and circumstances of the family.

6. Responsible Parenthood

Understanding parental responsibilities and family wellness.

Physical Needs

Providing appropriate care, protection, nutrition, shelter, and attention to children's basic needs.

Emotional Support

Providing care, guidance, respect, and a supportive family environment.

Education

Supporting children's learning, development, and responsible decision-making.

Financial Responsibility

Considering the family's resources and responsibilities when making important decisions.

Protection

Helping maintain a safe environment and protecting family members from preventable harm.

Guidance

Teaching values, responsibility, respect, and healthy decision-making.

7. Family Planning

Making informed and responsible family decisions.

Family planning is the process of making informed decisions about whether and when to have children and about the spacing of pregnancies.

It considers health, personal circumstances, family resources, responsibilities, and informed decision-making.

Key principle:

Family planning should involve accurate information, responsible decision-making, respect, and consideration of the well-being of everyone involved.

8. Broad Classifications of Family Planning

Know the broad categories and what they mean.

Natural Methods

Approaches that use awareness of fertility patterns and naturally occurring reproductive signs.

Barrier Methods

Methods designed to prevent sperm from reaching an egg by creating a physical barrier.

Hormonal Methods

Methods that use hormones to prevent pregnancy through effects on reproductive processes.

Intrauterine Methods

Methods involving a device placed inside the uterus by a qualified healthcare professional.

Permanently Intended Methods

Medical procedures intended to provide permanent contraception.

Emergency Contraception

Options used after unprotected sex or contraceptive failure to reduce the chance of pregnancy.

Health reminder:

Specific family-planning choices involve individual health considerations. Reliable health information and qualified healthcare professionals should be used when making personal medical decisions.

9. Informed Family Decisions

What responsible decision-making involves.

01

Gather Accurate Information

Learn from reliable and evidence-based health sources.

02

Consider Responsibilities

Think about the responsibilities involved in caring for a family.

03

Consider Family Wellness

Consider the physical, emotional, social, and financial well-being of the family.

04

Make Responsible Choices

Decisions should be informed, responsible, respectful, and appropriate to the family's circumstances.

10. Family Wellness

How responsible decisions can support the whole family.

01

Physical Wellness

Supporting health, safety, nutrition, and access to appropriate healthcare.

02

Emotional Wellness

Maintaining supportive relationships, communication, and emotional care.

03

Social Wellness

Building healthy relationships and encouraging cooperation within the family and community.

04

Financial Wellness

Managing available resources responsibly while considering family needs and responsibilities.

11. Health Quick Review

Key concepts for your review.

Responsible Parenthood: Recognizing and fulfilling the responsibilities involved in raising and caring for children.
Family Planning: Making informed decisions about whether and when to have children and about pregnancy spacing.
Informed Decision: A decision based on accurate information and consideration of relevant circumstances.
Family Wellness: The overall well-being of family members, including physical, emotional, social, and financial aspects.
Responsible Choice: A decision that considers consequences, responsibilities, respect, and well-being.
